None of these is required. Each one removes a few clicks, and a few clicks times a few hundred days adds up. Pick the two that fit your work and ignore the rest until you want them.

![Four cards: inline editing, pinned list views, favorites with the star in the Salesforce header, and the shortcut menu.]({{ '/assets/images/power-user-tips.svg' | relative_url }})

## Inline editing

In a list view, click the pencil that appears at the right edge of a cell, or double-click the cell, and edit it where it sits. Change several cells, then save. This is the fast way to fill in a column of blank LMS values for schools you've just talked to. Calculated fields don't offer the pencil, which is on purpose.

## Pinned list views

Salesforce opens an object's tab on the list view you used last. To make a particular one the default, open the list view's menu and choose **Pin this list view**. If you built "My A/B Fit Schools" ([Finding Things]({{ '/modules/06-finding-things/' | relative_url }})), pin it and Accounts opens to your day's work.

## Favorites

The **star in the Salesforce header** (top right, in Salesforce itself, not your browser's bookmark star) saves the page you're on. Records, list views, reports, and dashboards can all be favorites. Open the favorites list from the same header to jump back to any of them.

Favorite the five things you open every week: your list view, a Case queue, the one school you're nursing along. Skip the rest.

## The shortcut menu

Press **Ctrl + /** (**Cmd + /** on a Mac) on any page to see the keyboard shortcuts available on that page. The list changes depending on where you are, so open it where you work and pick up the one or two that help.

## Record URLs

Every record has its own web address, and the record's ID is part of it. Copy the address from your browser and paste it in Slack, a ticket, or Chatter, and the other person lands on exactly that record (if they have access). It's better than "search for Cedar Bluff and open the second one."

Use links to point at records. Don't paste the record's contents into a message when the contents include student or donor details.

## Preview a record from a report

Since the Winter '27 release (October 2026), you can preview a record from inside a report. Click the **eye icon** next to a row to open a preview panel, then close it and keep reading. It saves the click-open-click-back loop when you're checking ten rows.

## Account hierarchy

Districts are parents of their schools: Willow Creek Unified School District is the parent of Willow Creek High School. Some college systems are parents of campuses. On a school's record, click the **Parent Account** link to go up. From the district you can see every school under it. Hierarchies were cleaned up in fall 2026, so they're reliable for K12 landscape questions.

## School brief

On your Home page (if your role has it) the **School brief** component says "Brief me on a school". Pick a school and get a one-screen summary. Use it five minutes before a call with Ms. Dana Brooks at Willow Creek High School to get your bearings, then open the record for the detail.

## The mobile app

The Salesforce mobile app is good for looking up a record before a meeting or logging a quick note after a call. It's less suited to long data entry or building reports. Use it for what it does well.
