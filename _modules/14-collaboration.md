Between Slack, email, meetings, and whatever else your team uses to communicate, the last thing you need is another communication tool. And Salesforce's built-in collaboration features aren't trying to replace any of those. What they offer is something different: **contextual communication that stays attached to the thing you're communicating about**.

### Chatter: Conversations That Stick to Records

**Chatter** is Salesforce's internal communication feed. You can post on any record, @mention colleagues, share files, and have threaded conversations. It works like a simplified version of Slack.

The key difference from Slack and email: when you post on an Account's Chatter feed, that conversation is *permanently attached to that Account*. Six months later, when someone else opens that Account, they can scroll through the Chatter feed and see the full discussion history. "IT team confirmed they support Canvas integration, but the department chair wants to wait until fall." "Marketing sent the Assignable overview deck to Dr. Park." "Support escalated the access code issue — resolved, batch was expired."

That context doesn't exist in Slack, where it's buried in a channel nobody will think to search. It doesn't exist in email, where it's scattered across inboxes. It exists on the record, where it belongs.

### When to Use Chatter vs. Other Tools

A simple framework:
- **Slack** → Real-time coordination, quick questions, watercooler conversations
- **Email** → External communication, formal correspondence, anything leaving the organization
- **Chatter** → Internal discussions *about a specific record* that should be findable later

![When to use what: Slack is real-time but ephemeral (buried in channels within days). Email is for external communication but scattered across inboxes. Chatter is permanent, attached to the record, and findable by anyone forever.]({{ '/assets/images/slack-email-chatter.svg' | relative_url }})

The best Chatter habit is simple: when you learn something about an Account, Contact, or Opportunity that a colleague might need later, post it on the record's Chatter feed. It takes thirty seconds and creates a permanent, searchable note that's exactly where someone would look for it.

### Following Records

You can "follow" any record in Salesforce. When you follow an Account or Contact, you'll get notifications in your Chatter feed whenever that record is updated, has a new post, or has a significant field change. This is useful for keeping tabs on key accounts or high-priority deals without having to manually check the record every day.
